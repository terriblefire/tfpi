The TF4060's Pi serial port: from early boot on, the SSBL's boot log and debug output that goes through exec's RawPutChar (kprintf, Enforcer), and the TF4060 support Shell; what you type reaches that Shell. It needs FPGA firmware with the port, and a314d with it on this Pi. On the Amiga the port is the console device TFCON:: anything sent there shows up here (for example list >TFCON:).

It is not the Amiga's serial port: DiagROM and anything that uses serial.device (terminal programs, a Shell on AUX:) do not show up here.
